The AI Fluency Framework for Healthcare Professionals

A 15-minute interactive explainer for clinicians, public health, nursing, pharmacy, laboratory and allied colleagues. No technical background needed. Works offline.

AI fluency is the ability to work with AI effectively, efficiently, ethically and safely.

The framework was developed by Prof Rick Dakan and Prof Joseph Feller. This page is an unofficial teaching summary with healthcare examples.

The four competencies: the 4Ds

Tap a D to jump to it. A simple way to remember the order: Decide, Explain, Check, Own.

Three ways of working with AI

The framework describes three modes of human–AI interaction. Fluency includes choosing the right mode for the task.

Inside each D

Each competency has three sub-skills. Open them one at a time.

Try it

1. Delegation: who should do this task?

For each task, choose how you would work. There is often more than one defensible answer, so read the reasoning.

2. Description: build a better prompt

Describe the product (what you want), the process (how to get there) and the performance (how the AI should behave).

Your combined prompt appears here.

Never paste patient names, ID numbers or other identifying details into a public AI tool.

3. Discernment: spot what is wrong

An AI assistant answered: "How should a child with acute watery diarrhoea be managed?" Tap every sentence you would not accept without checking, then press Check.

4. Diligence: your personal AI-use commitments

Tick the commitments you will make. Your choices are saved on this device only.
